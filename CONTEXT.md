# Visualang

Turning spoken audio sources into illustrated, replayable visualizations that give language learners contextual comprehensible input.

## Language

**Visualization**:
The illustrated, audio-synced artifact a user creates from a source, exported as a video with its transcript and images. The primary action verb is "Visualize".
_Avoid_: Lesson, story, storybook

**Source**:
The YouTube link or uploaded audio file a visualization is made from.
_Avoid_: Lesson, input material

**Scene**:
One illustrated moment within a visualization, synced to a span of the transcript.
_Avoid_: Frame, slide

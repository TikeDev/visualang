# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Language learners who use spoken video or audio as comprehensible input. They may work on desktop or mobile and need to turn any audio source into a visual, replayable visualization without managing a complex media-production workflow.

## Product Purpose

Visualang turns a YouTube source or uploaded audio file into a transcript-driven illustrated visualization. It extracts concrete visual moments, generates storybook imagery, provides a synchronized preview, and exports the finished video with its transcript and images. Success means the user can follow long-running processing with confidence, review the result, and leave with a useful downloadable visualization that gives their audio contextual comprehensible input.

## Vocabulary

The created artifact is a **visualization**; the primary action is **Visualize**. The input is a **source**; each illustrated moment is a **scene**. Never "lesson". See CONTEXT.md for the full glossary.

## Brand Personality

Focused, crisp, humane.

The experience should feel like a well-made tool: intelligent, calm under load, and quiet enough that the generated imagery provides all the warmth. Its language is direct and reassuring without presenting the system as magical.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI startup interfaces built around purple gradients, cosmic glows, sparkles, or "magic" language
- The warm-editorial AI formula: cream canvas, serif display type, terracotta accents
- Chatbot, prompt-playground, agent-dashboard, and token-pill interaction models
- Floating glass cards, empty center-column SaaS heroes, and decorative metrics that obscure the workflow
- Generic content-generation framing that makes language learning or the exported video feel secondary
- Noisy progress messaging, low-contrast controls, or motion that competes with the user's task

## Design Principles

1. **Make the visualization the outcome.** Emphasize what the user is making and the downloadable video rather than the underlying AI pipeline.
2. **Keep the workflow unmistakable.** The pipeline stepper (Source → Transcript → Scenes → Video) is the spine of the interface; every state change reads against it.
3. **Earn trust during waiting.** Long-running steps need concise progress, useful status, and recoverable failures without visual noise.
4. **Speak to learners, not developers.** Copy names what the user recognizes; technical diagnostics go to the console, never the interface.
5. **Let the imagery carry the character.** The chrome stays cool and quiet so the generated scenes are the identity of the product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard operation, visible high-contrast focus states, readable text contrast in both themes, semantic headings, status announcements, and layouts that do not rely on color alone. State changes move focus deliberately, file upload remains clearly operable, and every non-essential animation has a reduced-motion alternative. Desktop and mobile experiences are both required, and light and dark themes are both first-class.
